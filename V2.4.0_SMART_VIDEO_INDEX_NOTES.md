# PixMind v2.4.0 — Smart Video Index (Experimental)

Built on the v2.3.9 app-level Smart Index candidate. Photo AI models, Face v3 thresholds/clustering, OCR engines, visual embeddings, Face Sticker, and existing photo search behavior are intentionally unchanged.

## Video indexing toggle

Search → Settings → Smart Index now includes **Smart video indexing**.

- ON (default): Complete Smart Index also samples videos for objects and known people.
- OFF: all video AI work is skipped, giving the fastest photo-only indexing pass.
- Changing the switch never invalidates completed photo work. Turning it back on later continues only videos missing the current video pipeline versions.

## Video v1 pipeline

Video processing is integrated into the existing five-stage Smart Index rather than exposed as a sixth independent pipeline.

### Content understanding

After missing photo Content work is complete, video frames are sampled adaptively:

- up to 30 s: approximately every 2 s
- 30–120 s: approximately every 3 s
- over 120 s: approximately every 5 s
- hard cap: 60 sampled frames per video

Frames are extracted locally on Android with `MediaMetadataRetriever` through the `pixmind/video_frame` MethodChannel and resized to at most 640×640 JPEG. No FFmpeg runtime is added.

YOLO11n runs on sampled frames. Object hits are aggregated per video with the first matching timestamp and a hit count. Frames where YOLO sees a person are retained as candidates for the People phase.

### People

After the normal photo Face v3 stage has built/updated stable People clusters, only person-candidate video frames are revisited (maximum 24 per video). MobileFaceNet/Face v3 matching is used conservatively against existing visible/named People.

Video frames **do not create new People clusters and do not update Face Lab centroids/prototypes**. This protects the tested photo clustering accuracy from repeated/noisy video frames.

## Search

Local federated search now supports video ids for:

- objects
- known people
- general text terms that match either of the above

The existing Search in control works as follows:

- Photos: existing photo indexes only
- Videos: Smart Video Index v1 only
- All: unions photo and video sources inside each clause, then keeps the existing cross-clause intersection behavior

Video results display a play icon. OCR/color/scene/date video indexing is intentionally not part of v1.

## Resume / safety

- Completed videos are persisted in SQLite with independent object and People pipeline versions.
- Re-running Complete Smart Index skips completed videos.
- Stop safely preserves completed videos; an interrupted current video may be retried from the start on the next run.
- Smart Index remains app-level, so it continues while switching tabs. This experimental video pass is not yet a full WorkManager process-death-persistent chain.

## Database additions

- `video_index_state`
- `video_object_hits`
- `video_person_candidate_frames`
- `video_person_hits`

Person hits store `person_id`, not a copied name, so renaming a Person immediately changes video name search. Manual person merges also migrate video references to the surviving Person.
